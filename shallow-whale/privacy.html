Shallow Whale privacy policy

Effective 3 October 2026

Shallow Whale is a crypto trading game played with play money. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no purchases. It does not collect, store or share any personal information about you.

What stays on your device

Your game progress and settings (cash, holdings, quests, theme and price source) are saved on your device only, in the browser's or the app's local storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

To delete them, use Reset everything in Settings, clear the site's data in your browser, or uninstall the app.

Live prices

By default the game shows real market prices. To get them, your device asks one of these providers for current prices and 24 hour trading volume about every 15 seconds, and for each coin's prices over the last 24 hours about every 5 minutes:

  • Binance (data-api.binance.vision), and if that fails,
  • CoinGecko (api.coingecko.com).

These requests contain only the list of coins. They carry nothing about you or your game. Like any request on the internet, they show the provider your IP address and basic device details such as your browser version, and the provider's own privacy policy covers how it treats them: Binance, CoinGecko.

To stop these requests, choose the simulated market in Settings. The game then makes up its own prices on your device and sends nothing.

The web version

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log the IP address of visitors, as described in the GitHub privacy statement. The game's fonts are bundled with it, so loading the game sends nothing to font services such as Google Fonts.

Children

The game collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes

If what the game does with data ever changes, this page changes first, with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the site's GitHub page.
