For two years I managed an IoT squad. Now I direct AI agents: coding agents that write the code, discovery agents that research a question, and agents that present the results. The habits carry over more than I expected.

## What managing a squad taught me

Most problems were not about skill. They came from an unclear goal, missing context, or work that nobody reviewed until it was too late.

## Agents need the same things

A written task with a clear finish line. The context they cannot guess: the rules of the project, what not to touch, how to check the result. A check they must pass before I look. And a review before anything is merged.

## What is different

An agent starts every task from zero, so whatever I want it to remember has to be written down. It also rarely pushes back on a bad plan unless I ask it to. Both make the manager's job more important, not less.
