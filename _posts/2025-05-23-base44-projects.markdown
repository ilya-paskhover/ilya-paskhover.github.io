A couple of small apps I built with [Base44](https://base44.com), an AI-powered app builder that lets you go from idea to working web app very quickly.

1. [Interactive/AI CV](https://ilyas-ai-cv.base44.app) — A conversational interface on top of my resume. Ask it questions about my experience and it answers using the actual content of my CV.
2. [Epoch Converter](https://epochconverter.base44.app/) — A simple Unix timestamp converter with a clean UI. Converts between epoch seconds and human-readable dates.
