## No ads, no purchases, no real money

The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no purchases. Your progress and settings stay on your device. The [privacy policy](/shallow-whale/privacy.html) has the details.

## Play it

It runs in the browser and can be installed as an app from the browser menu.
